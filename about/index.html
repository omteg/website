About OMTEG LTD

Corporate delivery excellence built around safety, compliance, operational discipline, and long-term value creation for Nigeria's energy industry.

Company Overview

OMTEG LTD is a Nigerian company providing logistics, marine support, procurement, engineering, and project services to oil and gas operators and contractors.

Mission

To deliver safe, efficient, and accountable support solutions that strengthen production continuity and project outcomes across the energy value chain.

Vision

To be a leading indigenous partner recognized for operational integrity, technical reliability, and sustainable growth.

Values

  • Safety and environmental responsibility
  • Integrity and transparency
  • Service excellence
  • Continuous improvement

Local Content Commitment

OMTEG aligns operations with NCDMB expectations through Nigerian workforce development, local supplier participation, and local capability transfer.
