Company Profile

OMTEG is built for offshore execution. We support deepwater energy operations with marine logistics, vessel capability, engineering oversight, project management, compliance control, and AI-supported planning.

About OMTEG

OMTEG is an offshore logistics and marine services company supporting Tier-1 operators, service companies, and field contractors across global deepwater markets.

Our operating model is built around vessel readiness, marine assurance, technical coordination, and disciplined execution. We support rig moves, FPSO interface work, cargo resupply, subsea support, towage, crew transfer, and marine project delivery with a safety-first approach.

Mission

To deliver reliable offshore logistics, marine operations, engineering support, and project management services that keep critical energy assets moving safely and on schedule.

Operational Footprint

OMTEG serves offshore terminals, rig locations, FPSO field zones, coastal support bases, and port interfaces across international upstream and marine operating environments.

Company Profile Highlights

Offshore Logistics Services

Cargo movement, resupply, rig support, marine coordination, and vessel deployment for offshore operations.

Vessel Capabilities

DP2 AHTS, PSV, MPSV, tugboats, CTV/FSV, and flat top barges mobilised to mission needs.

Engineering Support

Marine engineering oversight, structural review, stability assessment, maintenance support, and technical compliance.

Project Management Support

Offshore planning, stakeholder coordination, vendor control, reporting, and schedule assurance.

AI-Enhanced Operations

Predictive maintenance, scheduling optimisation, digital twin rig move simulations, automated compliance tracking, and real-time dashboards.

Compliance

NIMASA, the Cabotage Act, NCDMB, ISM/ISPS, and IOC marine assurance frameworks.
