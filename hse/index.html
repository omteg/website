Compliance & Marine Assurance

OMTEG executes offshore operations with a compliance-first posture built for marine assurance, regulatory control, and safety-led discipline.

Compliance as an Operating Standard

Compliance is built into planning, mobilisation, execution, reporting, and closeout. The objective is to keep every marine movement auditable, authorised, and operationally safe.

OMTEG supports Tier-1 IOC expectations through structured marine assurance, vessel readiness checks, HSE integration, permit discipline, and controlled offshore execution across international operating environments.

Compliance Framework

A controlled operating framework aligned to applicable maritime requirements and IOC marine assurance processes.

01

NIMASA and Cabotage

Marine operations are planned and executed in line with applicable maritime requirements and local-content obligations where relevant.

02

NCDMB and Local Content

Operational participation, local supplier integration, and workforce planning support market-specific content expectations.

03

ISM / ISPS

Marine safety management, security controls, and documented operating procedures support vessel and terminal assurance.

04

IOC Marine Assurance

We align marine execution to the assurance expectations commonly applied by TotalEnergies, Shell, Chevron, and SEPLAT.

05

HSE Integration

Operational planning incorporates job hazard analysis, permit control, toolbox talks, and stop-work authority.

06

Operational Reporting

Compliance reporting, vessel status updates, incident tracking, and audit support remain available to client teams.

Assurance Deliverables

OMTEG supports document control, permit logs, marine checklists, incident reporting, and closeout records for offshore campaigns.
