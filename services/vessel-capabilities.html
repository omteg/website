Vessel Capabilities

Vessel classes configured for offshore logistics, cargo movement, rig support, and deepwater project execution.

Supported Vessel Classes

DP2 AHTS

120-180T bollard pull vessels for rig move support, anchor handling, towing, and FPSO mooring campaigns.

PSV

Platform supply vessels configured for liquid mud, brine, base oil, dry bulk, and deck cargo transport.

MPSV

Multi-purpose support vessels for subsea intervention, ROV support, offshore logistics, and accommodation support.

Tugboats

Harbour and offshore tug capability for towage, standby duties, and controlled marine movement.

CTV / FSV

Crew transfer and fast support vessels for personnel movement, light cargo, and urgent field mobilisation.

Flat Top Barges & Work Barges

Barge solutions for project cargo, equipment staging, marine works, and offshore support activities.

Operational Fit

Vessel selection is matched to mission profile, sea state, cargo load, and offshore interface requirements to support safe and efficient execution.

Deployment Support

OMTEG aligns vessel deployment with marine assurance expectations, HSE controls, and offshore project schedules.

We support charter planning, mobilisation readiness, operational coordination, and reporting so vessel activity stays controlled from dispatch to demobilisation.
