Offshore Execution Profile for Marine and Logistics Support.

OMTEG delivers marine operations, vessel support, engineering, project management, compliance, and AI-supported logistics for the global offshore energy sector.

Company Overview

OMTEG is an offshore logistics and marine services company supporting Tier-1 operators, contractors, and field teams with disciplined vessel operations, technical coordination, and marine assurance.

Core Offshore Logistics Competencies

Offshore cargo and resupply, rig move support, marine coordination, deck cargo handling, towage, standby response, and subsea logistics interface.

Vessel Classes Supported

DP2 AHTS, PSV, MPSV, tugboats, CTV/FSV, and flat top barges matched to mission profile and field requirements.

Engineering & Project Management Support

Marine engineering oversight, mooring and towing engineering, structural and stability assessment, offshore planning, vendor control, and operational reporting.

AI-Enabled Operational Enhancements

Predictive maintenance, vessel scheduling optimisation, digital twin rig move simulations, automated compliance tracking, and real-time operational dashboards.

Compliance

OMTEG aligns marine execution with the controls expected by major operator assurance teams and relevant maritime regulatory authorities.

  • NIMASA: Marine regulatory alignment and vessel operating discipline.
  • Cabotage and Local-Content Controls: Compliance discipline where local-content requirements apply.
  • Local-Content Expectations: Operational participation and supplier integration aligned to market requirements.
  • ISM / ISPS: Safety management, security controls, and audit-ready operating procedures.
  • Operator Assurance: Operational readiness aligned to leading energy company expectations.
